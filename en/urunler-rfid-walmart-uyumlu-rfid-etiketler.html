Walmart-Compliant RFID Labels

MOOVE Self-Adhesive UHF RFID Labels are designed for item-level identification of retail products to be shipped to Walmart USA. Operating in the FCC 902–928 MHz band, the labels combine printed data such as product information and barcodes with the EPC encoded on the chip. Goods receipt, stock counting and shipment control can be carried out with compatible UHF RFID readers.

We offer MV-4350 for small product boxes, MV-4351 for long, narrow packaging surfaces, MV-4352 where a larger print area is needed for product information, barcodes and QR codes, and MV-4353 for large cardboard boxes. The models are supplied as finished labels with a coated paper print surface and hot-melt adhesive.

MV-4350

MOOVE Compact Self-Adhesive UHF RFID Product Label

MV-4350 is a 45 × 18 mm self-adhesive UHF RFID label for small product boxes and packaging with limited print area. With its 80 g/m² coated face, hot-melt adhesive and Impinj M830 chip, it is designed for product tracking applications in the FCC 902–928 MHz band used in the USA.

View
MV-4351

MOOVE Slim Strip Self-Adhesive UHF RFID Packaging Label

Measuring 73 × 20 mm, MV-4351 is a self-adhesive UHF RFID label developed for packaging with a long, narrow label area. With its 80 g/m² coated paper face, hot-melt adhesive and Impinj M830 chip, it is used in product tracking applications for the FCC 902–928 MHz band used in the USA.

View
MV-4352

MOOVE Self-Adhesive UHF RFID Retail Product Label

Measuring 54 × 35 mm, the MV-4352 is a self-adhesive UHF RFID label offering a larger print area for product information, barcodes and QR codes. With its 80 g/m² coated paper face, hot-melt adhesive and Impinj M830 chip, it is used for retail product tracking in the FCC 902–928 MHz band used in the USA.

View
MV-4353

MOOVE Wide Self-Adhesive UHF RFID Packaging Label

Measuring 97 × 25 mm, the MV-4353 is a self-adhesive UHF RFID label for large cardboard packaging and product boxes. Made with an 80 g/m² coated paper face and hot-melt adhesive, the label supports product tracking in the FCC 902–928 MHz band used in the USA with its Impinj M830 chip.

View

MOOVE Labels Tailored to Your Application

We can supply the labels printed with your company logo, product code, serial number, barcode or QR code, or unprinted. We match the EPC encoding to your product records and determine the label's position on the packaging using a sample. For the Walmart programme, an approved inlay is selected according to the ARC requirement for the relevant product category, and a finished packaging sample is submitted for approval. Category-specific Walmart compliance for the four models shown in the image is confirmed on a project basis.
